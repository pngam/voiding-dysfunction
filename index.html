Model Details

Formula: P(voiding dysfunction) = 1 / (1 + e−z)

z = −2.0122 + 0.0235 × age − 0.0444 × BMI + 0.0963 × lesion size + 0.2120 × parametrial length + 0.5479 × vaginal cuff length

Validation AUC 0.691 (95% CI 0.627–0.753)
Calibration Slope 1.19
Brier Score 0.212 (BSS = 0.07)
Development Cohort n = 701 (329 events, 46.9%)
Validation Cohort n = 319 (111 events, 34.8%)
Research Tool — For Research Use Only

This calculator implements the 5-variable LASSO model from a single-center temporal validation study (n=1,020). It has not been externally validated at other institutions. Do not use as the sole basis for clinical decision-making. All patient management decisions should be made by qualified healthcare professionals based on comprehensive clinical assessment.
